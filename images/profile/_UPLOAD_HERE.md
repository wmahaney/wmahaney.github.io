# UPLOAD HERE: profile photo

Put your profile photo in this folder, named exactly **`profile.jpg`**.

- Portrait orientation works best (the home page frame is 4:5, about 600 x 750 px or larger).
- Keep it under ~500 KB (resize large camera photos first).
- Until `profile.jpg` exists, the home page shows an empty frame labelled "Profile photo".
- If your photo is a PNG, convert it to JPG or tell Claude to change the filename in `index.qmd`.
