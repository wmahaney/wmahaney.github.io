# UPLOAD HERE: images for Blog posts

Pictures used in blog posts go here (PNG, JPG, SVG).

In a post, include one with:

```
![Short description](../../../images/blog/my-picture.jpg)
```

(The `../../../` climbs from `blog/posts/<post-name>/` to the site root.)

To use a picture as the thumbnail on the Blog card, add `image: ../../../images/blog/my-picture.jpg`
to the post's header.
