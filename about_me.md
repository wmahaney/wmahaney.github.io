I am a PhD student at Virginia Tech specializing in computational algebra and number theory and related areas. My work focuses on computational and probabilistic questions that arise from studying phenomena related to supersingular elliptic curves and supersingular isogeny graphs.

I have programming experience in Python3, C, C++, SageMath, Magma, LaTeX, Git, Linux. I like implementing algorithms and data structures and solving mathematical problems in code.

Beyond work I enjoy bouldering, playing Magic the Gathering, playing video games, and spending time with animals.
